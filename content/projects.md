## fantasy football

![fantasy football charts](/projects/fantasy_football_2021_combined_charts_50pct.png)

* project link: https://github.com/joeeoj/fantasy-football
* nbviewer link: https://nbviewer.org/github/joeeoj/fantasy-football/tree/main/

Never ending project to get better at NFL fantasy football. Got a chance to do some data wrangling and analysis along the way and get better at altair and mkdocs for writing [project documentation](https://joeeoj.github.io/fantasy-football/).

Results:

* 2020: Second to last
* 2021: 3rd
* 2022: 1st
* 2023: 5th
* 2024: 6th (and I missed playoffs by one spot because of weird league rules)
